HomeWire Lab - Support

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Need help with HomeWire Lab, found a bug, or have a suggestion? We are happy to help.

Contact

Email: feijoallc@gmail.com. Please include your device model, Android or iOS version, the app version, and a short description of what happened (a screenshot helps). We aim to reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I change the language? Open the language option in the app. English, Arabic, Spanish and French are available, and Arabic switches the layout to right-to-left.

What does "Request help" do? It plays an optional rewarded video ad, after which the lesson's hints are unlocked.

The ad did not load. Check your internet connection and try again in a moment. Ads may be unavailable in some regions.

How do I change my ad consent choice? Use the Privacy (i) button in the side toolbar.

I lost my progress. Progress is stored only on your device, so uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it. We cannot recover it because we do not keep copies.

Is this a replacement for an electrician? No. The app is for learning only. Always follow your local wiring rules and use a licensed electrician for real work.
